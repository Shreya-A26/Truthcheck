METRICS IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Understanding Academic Evaluation Terminology

Accuracy (99.08%)

The percentage of total articles in the held-out test evaluation whose classification matched their actual ground truth label.

Precision (99.12%)

When the model classifies an article as credible, precision indicates how often that assessment is factually accurate.

Recall (99.04%)

The fraction of all authentic articles that the model successfully recognized without falsely flagging them as misleading.

F1-Score (99.08%)

The balanced harmonic mean between precision and recall, ensuring neither false alarms nor missed deceptive articles skew performance.

RESEARCH ETHICS & CRITICAL LIMITATIONS

Dataset Context & Out-of-Distribution Generalization

The underlying classifier was trained on curated reference corpora, including the widely-studied ISOT Information Security and Object Technology news dataset. While this benchmark establishes strong statistical convergence on reference wire news and documented fabricated exemplars, high test set accuracy on historical data does not guarantee equivalent accuracy on contemporary breaking news, satire, regional publications, or adversarial misinformation.

Academic Transparency Note: TruthLens is engineered as a decision-support and news literacy platform. Automated model inferences must always be interpreted alongside live journalistic evidence and original primary sources.
