02 / Perspective Information Dynamics

“A convincing headline is not the same as convincing evidence.”

Modern misinformation rarely presents itself as an obvious fiction. It flourishes in the quiet space between partial facts, omitted caveats, and simulated authority. A headline may be grammatically persuasive and emotionally resonant while remaining fundamentally unsupported by primary records.

Credibility analysis requires more than binary categorization: it demands looking at who reported it, what independent sources confirm it, and what crucial nuances were discarded in the pursuit of virality.
